PayLume · Demo screencast

Embedded Shopify admin app. Recorded on a development store with fictional data (1 min 46 s).

What the screencast shows:

  1. Dashboard: reconciliation health, reconciled and unmatched order value, and a daily chart.
  2. “Run reconciliation” matches Shopify orders against imported payment settlements.
  3. Needs attention: the exception queue of orders with missing or mismatched payments.
  4. Reconciliation and Settlements: matched orders and provider payouts.
  5. Alerts: open, critical and warning alerts with Investigate and Dismiss actions.
  6. CSV import and Data sources: bring in settlement files from payment providers.
  7. Billing: the active plan, monthly order usage and plan changes through Shopify.
